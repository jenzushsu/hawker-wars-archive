What happens when AI runs a hawker stall? 🍚

Four policies. One lunch rush. Decisions about cooking, restocking, serving—and when to wait.

In this run of Hawker Wars, hand-written rules still earned the most. But the more interesting result was a stall that ran out of food and kept choosing WAIT, even with money and time to restock.

Describing the actions more clearly didn't change that choice. Asking the model to choose a complete short plan did—and the choice held when I reversed the option order in repeated diagnostic tests.

Does that make it a better stall operator? That's the next test.
